4. Who we share it with

We use these service providers. They process data only to provide their service:

  • Vercel Inc. (USA): hosting and network security.
  • Upstash Inc. (database in the USA, region us-east): storing account data, settings and activity.
  • Google LLC (USA): sign-in with Google.
  • Resend Inc. (USA): sending emails (your email address, name and the content of the email).
  • Paddle.com Market Limited (UK): our reseller and merchant of record. If you subscribe, Paddle collects your payment and billing details as an independent controller under Paddle's privacy notice.

Market data comes from Alpaca and news from Exa; we send them no personal data. We may disclose data if required by law or to a competent authority.

5. International transfers

Your data is stored and processed in the United States. Transfers from the EU, UK and Türkiye rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and, where the provider is certified, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. For transfers from Türkiye we rely on the safeguards in KVKK Article 9, including standard contracts. You can ask us for a copy of the relevant safeguards.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account, profile and saved settings: while your account exists. Deleted immediately when you delete your account.
  • Activity history: the most recent 1,000 actions per account and 10,000 across the Site; older entries are overwritten. Deleted with your account.
  • Free plan counters: 2 days.
  • Records of policy acceptance and subscription status: for the life of the account. Paddle keeps payment and invoice records for as long as tax law requires.

7. Emails

If you subscribe, we email you when each payment is received; these emails are part of the service. Product updates and offers are sent only if you agreed to them. Agreeing is optional and does not affect your access. You can withdraw at any time, free of charge, with the unsubscribe link in any such email or with Email updates in the account menu. We keep a record of your agreement and its withdrawal as proof.

8. Cookies

We use only cookies that are strictly necessary for the Site to work, so we do not ask for cookie consent:

  • os_session: keeps you signed in (7 days, secure, HTTP-only).
  • os_state: protects the Google sign-in against forgery (10 minutes).
  • os_admin: second-factor check for the site owner only (12 hours).

When you open the checkout, Paddle may set its own cookies for payment and fraud prevention, as described in Paddle's privacy notice. We use no advertising or third-party analytics cookies. The browser's local storage keeps your display preferences on your own device.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict and port your data, to object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to withdraw consent where we rely on it. Under KVKK Article 11 you can learn whether your data is processed and request information about it, learn the purpose and whether it is used for that purpose, know the third parties it is transferred to in Türkiye or abroad, request correction or deletion and that third parties be notified, object to a result against you arising solely from automated analysis, and claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

You can use two of these rights yourself from the account menu: Download my data (a JSON file of everything stored about you) and Delete account. For anything else, email info@optionscreener.org. We reply within 30 days, free of charge.

You can also complain to a supervisory authority: in the EU, the authority in your country; in the UK, the ICO; in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK, kvkk.gov.tr).

10. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS with HSTS). Sessions use signed, HTTP-only cookies. The admin area requires Google sign-in plus an authenticator code. Access is limited to the site owner. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

11. Changes

When this policy changes materially we update the version above and ask you to accept it again the next time you use the Site.
