Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This page explains what information optionscreener.org (the "Site") collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Google account information: When you sign in with Google, we receive only your email address and name (the openid, email and profile scopes). Your password never reaches the Site.
  • Content you save: Your saved filters, watched option contracts and stock watchlist are stored and linked to your email address.
  • Session cookie: A signed, HTTPS-only cookie keeps you signed in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use information

Information is used only to identify you and show you the settings you saved. It is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with third parties. Use of Google user data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Service providers

The Site is hosted on Vercel; saved settings are stored in an Upstash (Redis) database. Market data comes from Massive and news from Exa; no personal information is sent to either.

Retention and deletion

Your information is kept while your account is in use. To have your data deleted, email omercanyildirim1@gmail.com. You can also remove the Site's access from your Google account at any time.
