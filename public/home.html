Questions

What is Options Screener?

Options Screener is a web app for US stock and ETF options. It shows the live options flow (large trades, sweeps and blocks), full option chains with greeks, implied volatility and open interest, and a screener that filters every contract by volume, open interest, IV, delta and spread.

Is Options Screener free?

Yes. The free plan lets you open up to 3 symbols a day with full option chains, stock quotes and your watchlist. Pro costs $9.99 a month and adds unlimited symbols, the live options flow, the contract screener, charts and stock insights. You can cancel at any time and there is a 14-day money-back guarantee.

How current is the data?

Stock prices update during market hours. On the current data feed, option quotes are indicative and option trades can arrive about 15 minutes late. The app shows the time of the newest price at the top of the screen and labels delayed data.

What is options flow?

Options flow is the stream of option trades as they print. Watching it shows where large amounts of money are going: which tickers, calls or puts, which strikes and expiries, and whether trades were bought at the ask or sold at the bid.

What counts as unusual options activity?

A contract usually counts as unusual when its volume today is much larger than its open interest or its normal daily volume, especially when the trades are large, aggressive (at or above the ask) and in short-dated or out-of-the-money contracts.

Which markets are covered?

Listed US stocks and ETFs and their options, including SPY, QQQ, IWM and single stocks such as NVDA, TSLA, AAPL and AMD.

How do I cancel Pro?

Open the account menu in the app and choose Manage subscription and card. Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for and is not renewed.
