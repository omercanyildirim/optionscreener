Billing details

  • Price: Pro costs $9.99 (USD) per month. Any sales tax or VAT that applies in your country is included in that price.
  • Billing: you are charged when you subscribe and then automatically every month on the same date until you cancel.
  • Cancel anytime: open the account menu in the app and choose Manage subscription and card. After you cancel, Pro stays active until the end of the month you have paid for, and you are not charged again.
  • Refunds: full refund of any payment, first or renewal, if you ask within 14 days of that payment. See the Refund Policy.
  • Payment methods: card, PayPal, Google Pay and Apple Pay.
  • Who charges you: our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders and handles billing, invoices and customer service inquiries about payments.
  • Free plan: no card needed. It includes 3 symbols a day and stays free.

What you get

Options Screener is software for viewing US options market data: option chains with greeks and implied volatility, a contract screener, charts and a feed of large option trades. It is an information tool. It does not place trades, connect to brokerage accounts or give investment advice.
